Final Year Research (FYDP)

Explainable Reinforcement Learning for Clinical Decision Support

Healthcare AI | Offline RL | Explainable AI (XAI)

My thesis develops a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) for Type 2 Diabetes management using patient timelines from the MIMIC-IV database. The system uses a Longitudinal Health Trajectory Engine to track biomarker velocities, moving averages, and trend directions.

To avoid the "Imitation Trap" of standard supervised models, I implemented an Offline Discrete Conservative Q-Learning (CQL) agent to recommend proactive monitoring intervals. The system pairs this with a SHAP-based explainability module that converts complex Q-values into natural-language clinical justifications and feeds them into an interactive clinician-in-the-loop dashboard.
